Acme Coffee Company - Employee Handbook (sample)

Working hours: The store opens at 7:00 AM and closes at 9:00 PM, Monday to Saturday. On Sundays it is open from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM.

Leave policy: Full-time employees get 18 days of paid leave per year. Up to 5 unused days can be carried into the next year.

Uniform: Staff must wear the green Acme apron and closed-toe shoes. Name badges are required at all times.

Training: New employees complete a 2-week barista training program before working alone at the counter.
